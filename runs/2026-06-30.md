## 1. Hormozi item studied

**Speed-to-value / removing friction from the buyer's path.**
Source: "$100M Offers" Value Equation applications, via uplify.ai (hormozi-value-equation-made-easy
and alex-hormozi-offer-breakdown summaries; search 2026-06-30).

Takeaways:
1. Friction at onboarding predicts friction throughout. The first steps after "buy" are
   where intent leaks out, so pre-walk that path and make it feel frictionless before the click.
2. Easy always wins. Show a short, concrete sequence ("pay, get the email, open Day 1") so
   the path reads as already-solved, lowering the Effort & Sacrifice term.
3. Speed to value: name the fast first result. Collapse Time Delay by showing first value
   arriving in the same sitting, not "someday after onboarding."
4. Ready-to-use beats build-from-scratch: the system, templates, and prompts are handed over
   done, so the buyer's job is to run, not assemble.
5. Put the path at the moment of decision (after the guarantee, before the final CTA) so the
   last thing a hesitant buyer reads is how easy the next 10 minutes are.

Full notes appended to `runs/_hormozi-study-log.md`.

## 3. Concrete improvements (2 real)

1. **New "moment you buy" speed-to-value strip removes the invisible "what happens after I
   pay?" anxiety.** A hesitant buyer at the bottom of the page silently wonders whether buying
   means a wait, an onboarding call, or a setup delay. The strip answers it with a concrete
   3-step path that resolves in about a minute, with Day 1 startable tonight. *Why it converts:*
   collapses Time Delay (first value tonight) and Effort & Sacrifice (no assembly, handed over
   done) at the exact decision point, right after the risk reversal. Hormozi: friction at
   onboarding predicts friction throughout, so pre-walking a frictionless path raises perceived
   ease before the click.
2. **Matching "How do I get it after I pay?" FAQ + JSON-LD.** The same speed-to-value answer
   now also lives where logistics objections go looking, in the buyer's own words. *Why it
   converts / helps SEO:* honest instant-delivery framing shrinks Time Delay; mirrored into
   FAQPage structured data so it is eligible for FAQ rich-result treatment. Buyer-psychology
   principle: answer the objection at the place the buyer searches for it.
