## 1. Hormozi item studied

**Value Equation: Time Delay + Effort & Sacrifice (the bottom two).**
Source: "$100M Offers" Value Equation, via creatoreconomy.so and uplify.ai summaries
(search 2026-06-29). Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay x
Effort & Sacrifice).

Takeaways:
1. Pages over-invest in the top (dream + proof) and ignore the bottom; shrinking Time
   Delay and Effort raises value without touching price.
2. Time Delay = time to first result. Moving first value to "tonight" beats "in 5 days."
3. Effort & Sacrifice = hours, energy, stress, learning curve. State the real effort in
   small swallowable units so it reads as light, not scary.
4. Honesty also raises Perceived Likelihood: a believable "8 to 12 hours across five days"
   beats an unbelievable "effortless one-click."
5. Answer the effort/time objection where it fires: hero (first glance) and FAQ (when they
   go looking).

Full notes appended to `runs/_hormozi-study-log.md`.

## 3. Concrete improvements (3)

1. **Hero now answers the silent "how much of my life will this eat?" on first glance.**
   The subhead's last beat went from a flat audience tag ("For builders done planning") to
   a concrete effort reducer ("One focused sitting a day, and the thing in your head is
   live"). *Why it converts:* directly minimizes the Effort & Sacrifice term of the Value
   Equation and shrinks perceived Time Delay, while the honest "one sitting" framing also
   lifts Perceived Likelihood. The busy-professional avatar's #1 objection is time, and it
   now gets answered above the fold.
2. **Final CTA adds a speed-to-value cue at the moment of decision.** "Instant access,
   start Day 1 today" tells the buyer the clock starts now, not after some onboarding lag.
   *Why it converts:* collapses Time Delay to zero at the highest-intent point on the page;
   pairs with the existing risk-reversal so the decision feels both fast and safe.
3. **New time-commitment FAQ removes the biggest unspoken objection.** A busy buyer reads
   "5 days" and silently worries it means five days off work. The new FAQ reframes it as
   one to two hours a sitting, 8 to 12 hours total, with the AI doing the heavy lifting.
   *Why it converts / helps SEO:* honest effort-framing lifts believability (Perceived
   Likelihood) while shrinking Effort & Sacrifice; mirrored into FAQPage JSON-LD so it is
   also eligible for FAQ rich-result treatment. Buyer-psychology principle: answer the
   objection in the buyer's own words, at the place they go looking for it.
