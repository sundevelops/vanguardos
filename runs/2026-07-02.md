## 1. Hormozi item studied

**C.L.O.S.E.R.: the sales-script framework (Clarify, Label, Overview, Sell the vacation,
Explain away concerns, Reinforce the decision), mapped to landing-page copy order.**
Source: web search on Hormozi's C.L.O.S.E.R. framework (Accelerator University / Whatbox
Digital summaries, Hormozi's own framework thread; search 2026-07-02).

Takeaways:
1. C.L.O.S.E.R. is a live sales-call script, but the beat order maps cleanly to a one-page
   funnel: Clarify (pain) should be immediately followed by Label (identify the serious,
   right-fit buyer) BEFORE Overview (what's included) and Sell (the dream). Labeling early
   raises commitment going into the pitch; labeling after the buyer already saw the full
   offer is a weaker, redundant beat.
2. Auditing the live page found the Label beat (the "for you if / not for you if"
   qualification section) sitting after Overview (offer-stack) and after Brand Work proof,
   sandwiched between Brand Work and the Five-Day Journey (Sell): a C-O-Proof-L-S order, not
   C-L-O-S.
3. A second, weaker Label beat existed further down (the "Built For" avatar-tap section),
   effectively re-qualifying an already-qualified buyer. Once real Labeling happens early,
   a later avatar-selection UI should read as Sell (a personalized dream outcome), not a
   second qualification gate.
4. Sell the vacation should cost the buyer nothing to see: gating a personalized dream
   outcome behind a required click adds Effort for no reason once the buyer has already
   scrolled past two Label beats.
5. Full-section reorders are the highest-risk edit type for a "small, focused" daily task,
   so before moving anything, verified `.reveal` and section-scroll animations use
   per-element IntersectionObserver (order-independent) and that no other part of the page
   anchors to the moved section by DOM position.

Full notes appended to `runs/_hormozi-study-log.md`.

## 3. Concrete improvements (3)

1. **Moved the Label (qualification) section to run Clarify → Label → Overview instead of
   Clarify → Overview → Proof → Label → Sell.** *Why it converts:* per Hormozi's C.L.O.S.E.R.,
   labeling the buyer as a serious, right-fit prospect raises their commitment going into the
   pitch. Doing it after the buyer has already seen the full offer stack and brand-work proof
   wastes that leverage, the label lands too late to shape how the rest of the page is read.
   Moving it earlier also means a cold visitor who is clearly the wrong fit ("not for you if")
   self-selects out before investing time in the full value stack, an honest, non-manipulative
   use of qualification.
2. **Reframed the second (avatar-tap) section from re-qualification to personalized selling.**
   *Why it converts:* two qualification beats separated by the Five-Day Journey read as
   repetitive and slightly suspicious ("why are you asking who I am again?"). Relabeling it as
   an outcome-preview ("Tap your starting point, see your exact outcome") turns the same UI
   into a Sell-the-vacation beat, each click now delivers a personalized payoff rather than a
   second gate.
3. **Defaulted the personalized promise panel to render on load instead of requiring a click.**
   *Why it converts:* per the Value Equation's Effort & Sacrifice term, every unnecessary click
   between the buyer and the payoff lowers perceived value. The panel now shows the strongest,
   most-tested avatar promise (the locked "paralyzed solopreneur" hook already proven in the
   hero and SEO meta) immediately, and still invites exploration of the other four avatars.
